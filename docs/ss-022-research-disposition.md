# SS-022 Research Disposition

Status: **Lead Architect approved the documentation-only scope for Builder.**
Research is input, not an accuracy, safety, privacy, licensing, or release
clearance.

## Adopt

- A versioned, local-run protocol with separate supported-input and challenge
  cohorts, scenario-level denominators, exclusions, and uncertainty reporting.
- Independent blinded dual review of phase labels and landmark assessability,
  followed by recorded adjudication.
- Phase-error reporting against adjudicated labels; report missing and
  unreviewable events separately, especially impact.
- Agreement-oriented paired reporting for any future reference-based geometry
  study: signed bias, absolute error, dispersion, and availability rather than
  correlation alone.
- Source-specific consent, provenance, privacy, licensing, and maintainer
  approvals before future validation media is collected or used.

## Revise

- Describe the phase output as a provisional eight-sample review layout, not
  automatic phase detection.
- Separate geometry primitives from the four unimplemented metric payload slots.
- Treat landmark visibility as an eligibility/review observation, not accuracy
  confidence or biomechanical truth.

## Defer

- Numeric acceptance margins, a power calculation, gold-standard reference
  tooling, reviewer qualifications, real-person or third-party media, dense
  sampling, unsupported camera views, and public accuracy claims.

## Reject

- Treating synthetic or mannequin fixtures as real-world validation.
- Forced impact labels, arbitrary biological ranges, correlation-only claims,
  or repeated swings counted as independent participants.
- Unreviewed media, new fixtures, model assets, providers, dependencies, or
  runtime changes.
- Claims of guaranteed correctness, biomechanical proof, medical usefulness,
  professional coaching correctness, performance improvement, privacy,
  deletion, safety, legal, or compliance clearance.

Observability is intentionally unchanged: SS-022 changes documentation only.
